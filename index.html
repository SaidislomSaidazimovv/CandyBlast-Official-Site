Good to know

Questions before your first move?

Here are the details for the first Candy Blast mobile release.

When will Candy Blast be available?

The first Android and iPhone release is being polished now. Store links will appear here as soon as the builds are approved.

How many levels are included?

The first chapter contains 20 handcrafted levels across four worlds. More chapters can follow after the launch build is stable.

Can I play without an account?

Yes. Guest play stores progress on the device. Signing in adds cloud backup and cross-device progress.

Does it work offline?

Core gameplay is designed to keep working after the game has loaded. Sign-in, cloud sync and online features need a connection.
