When can I download Candy Blast?

We are preparing the first mobile release. Once the apps are approved, this site will link directly to their Google Play and App Store pages.

How many levels are in the first release?

The first chapter has 20 designed levels across four worlds.

Can I play without an account?

Yes. Guest play stores progress on your device. Signing in adds cloud progress.

Can I play offline?

Core play works after the game has loaded. Account sign-in and cloud sync require a connection.
