Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Information we use

Candy Blast uses account information supplied through email, Google or Apple sign-in to identify your account. We may store your display name, account email association and game progress, including completed levels, scores, stars, lives, boosters, rewards, profile choices and settings.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, restore progress across devices, protect account data, operate game features and provide support. Candy Blast does not request access to Google Drive, contacts, calendars or unrelated account services.

Guest play

If you play as a guest, game progress stays on the device unless you later connect an account. Removing the app or clearing its local data may remove guest progress.

Storage and security

Account and cloud progress data is stored through Supabase. Access controls are used so signed-in players can access their own progress. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but we limit collected data to what the game needs.

Account deletion

Account deletion controls and a support contact will be available before the public mobile release. Deletion requests will remove account-linked game data where legally permitted.

Children and changes

The release age rating and any child-directed requirements will be finalized with the store listings. This policy may change as analytics, purchases and support systems are finalized. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
